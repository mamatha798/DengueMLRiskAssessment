Blood report awareness

CBC Report Guidance

Learn what common CBC parameters mean. A doctor must interpret your actual report in clinical context.

🧪

Common CBC Parameters

  • Platelets: Help blood clot.
  • WBC: White blood cells involved in immune defence.
  • Haemoglobin: Protein that carries oxygen.
  • Haematocrit: Proportion of blood made up of red blood cells.

Understand a Report Value

This is an educational explanation, not an interpretation of your medical condition.

Do not use platelet count alone to decide whether dengue is mild or severe. Doctors consider symptoms, examination, trends in blood results and other clinical findings. Do not delay care while waiting for a blood test.
Nutrition awareness

Food & Hydration Guidance

General supportive nutrition information during illness. Follow your clinician's advice, especially if you have other medical conditions.

🥗

General Food Choices

  • Freshly prepared, balanced meals.
  • Rice, soft foods, vegetables and fruits as tolerated.
  • Protein-containing foods such as dal, eggs or other suitable options.
  • Small, frequent meals if appetite is low.
  • Safe drinking water and suitable fluids.
💧

Hydration & Caution

  • Drink fluids regularly as advised by your doctor.
  • Oral rehydration solution may be useful when recommended.
  • Avoid alcohol.
  • Avoid unverified remedies claimed to increase platelets.
  • Do not self-medicate with aspirin or ibuprofen when dengue is suspected.
Fluid needs vary. People with kidney, heart or other medical conditions may need individual fluid advice. Ask a healthcare professional.
Safety first

Emergency Warning Signs

Warning signs can occur during dengue, including around the time fever begins to settle.

Seek urgent medical care immediately if any of these warning signs occur. Do not wait for this website to assess the situation.
  • Severe abdominal pain or tenderness.
  • Persistent vomiting.
  • Bleeding from nose or gums.
  • Blood in vomit or stool.
  • Difficulty breathing.
  • Confusion, unusual drowsiness or restlessness.
  • Cold, clammy skin or severe weakness.
Call India Emergency 112

Why Monitor Weather?

Standing water can provide mosquito breeding sites. Weather conditions are one part of a wider public-health picture.

  • Empty water from containers regularly.
  • Cover water storage containers.
  • Clean coolers, trays and drains.
  • Use mosquito protection measures.
  • Follow local public-health advisories.

Weather conditions alone cannot establish dengue transmission or individual infection risk.

Privacy & Data Security

Health information should be handled carefully.

Minimise personal data

Do not enter names, phone numbers, addresses or other identifying information unless truly required.

Secure backend

Use authentication, HTTPS, access controls and appropriate database protection for real patient records.

Consent & deletion

Explain what data is collected, why it is collected, how long it is retained and how users can request deletion.

This website's browser records are stored on the device using local storage. Do not use this demonstration storage for confidential or real patient records.

Dengue Prevention

Prevent mosquito breeding and protect yourself from mosquito bites.

🪣

Remove Standing Water

🛏️

Prevent Mosquito Bites

Use suitable repellents, screens and protective clothing. Use mosquito nets when appropriate.

🏡

Community Action

Keep surroundings clean and report mosquito-breeding concerns to local authorities.
